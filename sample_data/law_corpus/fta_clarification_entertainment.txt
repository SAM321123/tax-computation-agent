FTA Corporate Tax Guide CTGDBD1 — Determination of Taxable Income, Section 8.4

Client gifts, hospitality, staff parties and similar costs incurred to entertain
customers, suppliers, shareholders or other business partners fall within the
scope of "entertainment, amusement or recreation expenditure" under Article 32 of
the Corporate Tax Law, and are therefore only 50% deductible, regardless of
whether the cost was a reasonable and ordinary cost of doing business. This
includes meals, accommodation, transportation, admission fees and the cost of
gifts provided in a client-relationship context. Staff welfare costs available to
employees generally (such as an annual staff party open to all employees, or
routine refreshments provided in the office) are not treated as entertainment
expenditure for this purpose and remain fully deductible as a staff cost, subject
to the general deductibility conditions in Article 28.
