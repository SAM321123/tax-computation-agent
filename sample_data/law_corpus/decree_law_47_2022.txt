Federal Decree-Law No. 47 of 2022 on the Taxation of Corporations and Businesses

Article 3
Corporate Tax Rate
1. Corporate Tax shall be imposed on the Taxable Income of a Taxable Person at the
   following rates:
   a. 0% (zero percent) on the portion of Taxable Income not exceeding AED 375,000.
   b. 9% (nine percent) on the portion of Taxable Income exceeding AED 375,000.
2. A Qualifying Free Zone Person shall be subject to Corporate Tax at the rate of 0%
   on Qualifying Income and 9% on Taxable Income that is not Qualifying Income, as
   further specified in Article 18 of this Decree-Law.

Article 18
Qualifying Free Zone Person
1. A Qualifying Free Zone Person shall benefit from a rate of 0% on its Qualifying
   Income, provided the conditions in this Article and any relevant Ministerial
   Decision are met, including maintaining adequate substance in the Free Zone,
   deriving Qualifying Income as specified by Cabinet Decision, and complying with
   the arm's length principle and transfer pricing documentation requirements.
2. Qualifying Income includes income derived from transactions with other Free Zone
   Persons that are Qualifying Free Zone Persons, and income derived from qualifying
   activities as specified in a Ministerial Decision, excluding Excluded Activities.

Article 22
Exempt Income
1. Dividends and other profit distributions received from a juridical person that
   is a Resident Person shall be exempt from Corporate Tax.
2. Dividends and other profit distributions received from a Participating Interest
   in a foreign juridical person shall be exempt, subject to meeting the
   Participating Interest conditions of ownership percentage and minimum holding
   period set out in a Cabinet Decision.

Article 23
Participation Exemption
1. A gain or loss arising from the transfer, sale, or other disposition of a
   Participating Interest shall be exempt from Corporate Tax, provided the
   Participating Interest conditions are satisfied at the time of disposal,
   including a minimum ownership interest of 5% held for an uninterrupted period
   of at least 12 months.

Article 28
Interest
1. Interest expenditure shall be deductible in the Tax Period in which it is
   incurred, subject to the general interest deduction limitation rule in Article 30
   and any specific interest limitation rules applicable to related party
   transactions.

Article 30
General Interest Deduction Limitation Rule
1. Net Interest Expenditure shall be deductible up to 30% (thirty percent) of the
   Taxable Person's EBITDA for the relevant Tax Period, adjusted for interest,
   depreciation and amortisation.
2. Notwithstanding paragraph 1, a Taxable Person may deduct Net Interest
   Expenditure up to AED 12,000,000 (twelve million dirhams) regardless of the
   30% of EBITDA limitation, being the de minimis threshold.
3. Any Net Interest Expenditure disallowed under this Article may be carried
   forward and deducted in the subsequent ten Tax Periods, subject to the same
   limitation.

Article 32
Entertainment Expenditure
1. Expenditure incurred for entertainment, amusement or recreation of customers,
   shareholders, suppliers or other business partners shall be deductible only to
   the extent of 50% (fifty percent) of such expenditure.
2. Fifty percent (50%) of entertainment, amusement and recreation expenditure is
   non-deductible in determining Taxable Income, regardless of whether the
   expenditure was incurred wholly and exclusively for the purposes of the
   Business.

Article 33
Non-Deductible Expenditure
1. The following expenditure shall not be deductible in determining Taxable
   Income:
   a. Donations, grants or gifts made to an entity that is not a Qualifying Public
      Benefit Entity.
   b. Fines and penalties, other than amounts awarded as compensation for damages
      or breach of contract.
   c. Bribes or other illicit payments.
   d. Dividends, profit distributions or benefits of a similar nature paid to an
      owner of the Taxable Person.
   e. Corporate Tax imposed on the Taxable Person under this Decree-Law.

Article 37
Tax Loss Relief
1. A Taxable Person may carry forward and use a Tax Loss to reduce the Taxable
   Income of subsequent Tax Periods, subject to satisfying the continuity of
   ownership and business conditions set out in this Decree-Law.
2. The Tax Loss used to reduce Taxable Income in any Tax Period shall not exceed
   75% (seventy-five percent) of the Taxable Income for that Tax Period before
   any Tax Loss relief, with any unused amount carried forward indefinitely
   subject to the same conditions.
