Cabinet Decision No. 73 of 2023 on Small Business Relief for the Purposes of Federal
Decree-Law No. 47 of 2022 on the Taxation of Corporations and Businesses

Article 1
Definitions
Revenue means the gross amount of income derived during a Tax Period, before
deducting any expenditure.

Article 2
Small Business Relief
1. A Resident Person may elect to be treated as not having derived any Taxable
   Income for a Tax Period, and shall accordingly not be required to calculate
   its Taxable Income for that period, where its Revenue for the relevant Tax
   Period and previous Tax Periods does not exceed AED 3,000,000 (three million
   dirhams) for each such Tax Period.
2. The election under paragraph 1 is not available to a Qualifying Free Zone
   Person or a member of a Multinational Enterprise Group as defined for the
   purposes of Cabinet Decision No. 44 of 2020 on Organising Reports Submitted by
   Multinational Companies.
3. The Revenue threshold in paragraph 1 shall apply for Tax Periods commencing
   on or after 1 June 2023 and ending on or before 31 December 2026.
