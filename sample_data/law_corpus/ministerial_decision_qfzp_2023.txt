Ministerial Decision No. 139 of 2023 on Qualifying Activities and Excluded Activities
for the Purposes of Federal Decree-Law No. 47 of 2022

Article 1
Qualifying Activities
For the purposes of Article 18 of the Decree-Law, Qualifying Activities include:
   a. Manufacturing of goods or materials.
   b. Holding of shares and other securities for investment purposes.
   c. Ownership, management and operation of ships.
   d. Reinsurance services that are subject to the regulatory oversight of the
      competent authority.
   e. Fund management services that are subject to the regulatory oversight of
      the competent authority.
   f. Wealth and investment management services that are subject to the
      regulatory oversight of the competent authority.
   g. Headquarter services to Related Parties.
   h. Treasury and financing services to Related Parties.
   i. Financing and leasing of aircraft, including engines and rotable
      components.
   j. Distribution of goods or materials from a Designated Zone to customers
      that resell such goods, or materials, or process or alter them.
   k. Logistics services.

Article 2
Excluded Activities
Excluded Activities, income from which will never be Qualifying Income even if
otherwise connected to a Qualifying Activity, include:
   a. Transactions with natural persons, except in relation to Qualifying
      Activities under sub-paragraphs (c), (d), (e), (f) and (i) of Article 1.
   b. Banking activities that are subject to the regulatory oversight of the
      competent authority.
   c. Insurance activities other than reinsurance.
   d. Finance and leasing activities other than those specified in Article 1.
   e. Ownership or exploitation of immovable property, other than Commercial
      Property located in a Free Zone where the transaction is with another
      Free Zone Person.
   f. Ownership or exploitation of intellectual property assets.
   g. Any ancillary activities that do not meet the de minimis threshold.
